Mandatory Safety Instruction

Do not enter the water or attempt submerged net cuts. Authorized and certified dive teams handle all underwater salvage. Shore volunteers handle dry sorting and spooling only.

nature

Environmental Objective

Categorizing monofilament nylon gillnets and separating high-density polyethylene (HDPE) lines salvaged from Bali and East Java shipping lanes. Material is pre-sorted for regional closed-loop recycling partners, preventing microplastic degradation.
