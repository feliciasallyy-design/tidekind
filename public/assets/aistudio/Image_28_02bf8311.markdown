## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, community-driven environmental ethos: authoritative yet deeply welcoming, grounded, and purpose-driven. Built for a multi-faceted user base—spanning commercial fishers, local volunteers, research scientists, and civic partners—the design balances maritime utility with human warmth. 

The aesthetic is a hybrid of Modern Clean and Tactile Environmentalism:
- Generous, breathable layouts anchored by crisp maritime geometry.
- Organic softness expressed through warm neutral backdrops and gentle pill-shaped interactive triggers.
- Utilitarian precision for reporting coordinates, salvage data, and gear classifications.
- High ethical transparency, avoiding visual gimmickry in favor of clarity, directness, and immediate legibility under outdoor sun or wet field conditions.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from coastlines, ocean depths, and maritime materials:

- **Primary (`#123B4A` - Deep Ocean Navy):** The bedrock tone. Applied to major typographic anchors, primary navigations, critical status headers, and authoritative containers.
- **Secondary (`#168C83` - Marine Teal):** The functional engine. Applied to primary calls to action, completed progress bars, active filters, and interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F08B70` - Coral Accent):** The alert and focus trigger. Reserved for urgent gear hazard warnings, critical map cluster highlights, abandoned net danger flags, and pending verification badges.
- **Surface Accents & Neutrals:**
  - `Canvas (`#F7F6F0` - Warm Off-White)`: Primary page background, eliminating stark glare and introducing organic warmth.
  - `Surface Alt (`#E8DDC8` - Sand Beige)`: Structured cards, elevated panels, and border accents.
  - `Muted Accent (`#B8DCCB` - Sea-Glass Green)`: Positive recovery confirmations, safe-status tags, and soft supportive chips.
  - `On-Surface (`#22292F` - Dark Charcoal)`: High-contrast body copy maintaining AA/AAA compliance against sand and off-white backdrops.

## Typography

The type scale combines the modern geometric precision of **DM Sans** for displays and UI controls with the high-legibility mechanical clarity of **Inter** for long-form data, field reports, and narrative content.

- **Display & Headlines:** Headings use heavy optical weights (`600` and `700`) to provide instant structure across dense mapping feeds, field log summaries, and reporting dashboards. Tight negative tracking keeps larger text punchy and unified.
- **Body & Data:** Inter runs across all narrative content and field reporting inputs. Normal letter spacing ensures effortless readability when scanning net classifications, GPS coordinates, or coastal depth metrics.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or semi-bold micro-copy (`label-sm`) enforces immediate recognition for field metrics (e.g., `EST. WEIGHT`, `DEPTH: 12M`, `HAZARD LEVEL`) without cluttering visual hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for field utility, responsive map panels, and multi-tiered dashboards:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin` of `3rem`, `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Max container width spans `1280px` for optimal reading width and controlled data density.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin-tablet` of `2rem`, `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Modular cards collapse from 3-up to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of `1rem`, `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Secondary controls collapse into sticky floating bottom sheets or bottom navigation drawers for thumb accessibility in marine field conditions.

Layouts emphasize vertical flow with ample breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between narrative thematic blocks, while data tables and gear tracking cards leverage compact intra-component gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize screen utility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via natural ambient light and soft physical layering rather than dramatic digital drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Page background `#F7F6F0`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` or `#E8DDC8` at 40% tint. Outlined with a soft `#22292F` stroke at 8% opacity. Elevation shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 59, 74, 0.05), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(18, 59, 74, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Sticky Controls & Active Cards):** Outlined with subtle stone borders. Elevation shadow: `0 8px 18px -4px rgba(18, 59, 74, 0.08), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(18, 59, 74, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Gear Incident Drawers):** Backdrop dim of `#123B4A` at 35% blur opacity. Elevation shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(18, 59, 74, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **pill-shaped** form factor for primary actionable items combined with soft, humanized radii for structural containers. 

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Badges, Search Bars, Sliders):** Full pill rounding (`9999px`) provides a friendly, welcoming touch experience that contrasts crisply against sharp data tables and photographic content.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Radius of `1rem` (`16px`) creates gentle boundaries that avoid clinical harshness.
- **Nested Inner Modules (Photo thumbnails, map preview windows):** Scaled down proportionally to `0.5rem` (`8px`) to preserve structural alignment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#168C83` (Marine Teal) background, `#FFFFFF` text, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), minimum 44px height for touch targets. Hover state shifts to `#123B4A` with smooth transition.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined `#123B4A` border (1.5px), `#123B4A` text, transparent background. Hover state introduces a 6% tint of `#168C83`.
- **Urgent / Hazard Alert:** Solid `#F08B70` (Coral) background, `#FFFFFF` text, reserved for "Report Lost Gear" or "Critical Hazard Logged" triggers.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Safe / Recovered:** `#B8DCCB` background with `#123B4A` text.
- **High Risk / Ghost Net Alert:** `#F08B70` background at 15% opacity with solid `#D95338` text.
- **In-Progress Retrieval:** Soft `#168C83` at 12% opacity with `#168C83` text.
- Built with `label-sm` typography, `space-xs` vertical padding, and `space-sm` horizontal padding.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Warm white `#FFFFFF` surface with a subtle `1px` `#E8DDC8` border. On focus, transitions to a 2px `#168C83` ring with zero vertical displacement. Minimum height `48px` to ensure effortless field input on mobile devices.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded `4px` (checkboxes) and full circle (radios), sized `20px` by `20px` within an expanded `44px` invisible tap container. Active state fills with `#168C83` featuring an SVG white check.

### Cards & Reporting Logs
- **Ghost Gear Sightings Card:** Base `#FFFFFF` background, layered with an outer border of `#E8DDC8`. Includes visual location tags, timestamp, reporter role badge, and a top-right coral accent indicator if gear is reported as drifting or actively entangling marine wildlife.

### Additional Domain Components
- **Coordinate Badge:** Monospaced coordinate visualizer inside a `#E8DDC8` (30% opacity) pill container with an instant "Copy Lat/Long" tap interaction.
- **Recovery Progress Gauge:** Multi-segment bar visualizing target retrieval metrics, filling sequentially from `#B8DCCB` through `#168C83`.
